Cruis'n USA for Dreamcast

A native Sega Dreamcast port of the Nintendo 64 version, running at a solid 30 frames a second, with VMU saving, Dreamcast cheat codes, the arcade trophy girl and online leaderboards at Cruisn.net. Choose your own Cruis'n USA ROM and this page builds the Dreamcast disc image (.cdi) for you, right here in your browser. Nothing to install, and nothing is uploaded: your ROM never leaves your computer.

What you need

  • Your own ROM of Cruis'n USA (USA) (Rev 1) for the Nintendo 64, from your own cartridge: a .z64, .n64 or .v64 file, or a .zip with one inside.
  • A desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). It takes about a minute.
