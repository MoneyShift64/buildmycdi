Build My CDI

Dreamcast ports of classic games, built from your own copy of the original. Choose your game files and the page makes the Dreamcast disc image (.cdi) for you, in your browser. Nothing to install, and your files never leave your computer.

Manic Karts

The 1995 PC kart racer, running natively on the Dreamcast. Needs your own PC CD-ROM as a BIN/CUE image.

Cruis'n USA

The Nintendo 64 arcade racer, running natively on the Dreamcast at 30 frames a second. Needs your own ROM of Cruis'n USA (USA) (Rev 1).
